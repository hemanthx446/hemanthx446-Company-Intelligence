## 1. Core Strategic Research Domains

The intelligence system prioritizes evidence gathering, signal detection, and opportunity scoring across the following domains:

### A. Enterprise Applications & Core ERP
- Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) landscapes and modernization initiatives.
- **SAP Ecosystem:** SAP S/4HANA (Cloud / On-Premise / Private Edition), SAP ECC migration paths, Clean Core architecture paradigms.
- **SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP):** Integration Suite, Extension Suite, SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC), and application development extensions.
- **Application Lifecycle Management:** SAP Cloud ALM, Solution Manager transition planning, DevOps in enterprise application environments.

### B. Manufacturing Digital Transformation & Operations
- Smart factory and Industry 4.0 modernization programs.
- **Shopfloor Execution Systems:** Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES), Computerized Maintenance Management Systems (CMMS), and SCADA architectures.
- **SAP–MES–OT Convergence:** Bi-directional integration between enterprise ERP layers and operational technology (OT) / programmable logic controllers (PLCs) / industrial IoT gateways.
- Production scheduling, batch management, plant performance monitoring, and OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) analytics.

### C. Enterprise Integration & Operational Intelligence
- Hybrid integration platforms, API management, and enterprise message queuing (Kafka, Solace, MQTT, REST, RFC).
- **Data Traceability & Compliance:** Track-and-trace systems, supply chain genealogy, regulatory compliance records (FDA, EMA, OSHA, ISO, ESG).
- **Operational Data Pipelines & Reporting:** Real-time shopfloor telemetry consolidation, enterprise data warehousing, operational KPI dashboards.

### D. Advisory, Architecture & Solution Consulting
- Solution architecture design, target state blueprinting, and technical roadmap development.
- Presales engineering, digital maturity assessments, business case modeling, and value-engineering frameworks.
- Technology vendor selection, RFx formulation, and systems integration partner evaluation.
